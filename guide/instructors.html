Replays for the key moments

Every fast library example ships a recording. A link of the form ?replay=examples/replays/ID.cprec with &step=N, &view=view and &lecture=1 opens it on a step, with the domain view alone and its notes as captions, for a projector.

  • The first refinement: guide-03-hit-clique, step 1: a surviving triangle becomes a clause that rules out every candidate keeping it. Open the replay
  • A refinement that says too much: guide-04-one-node, whose audit names the lost solution; show the step, then press Audit. Open the replay
  • A core shrinking: maxsat-ihs, whose cores are made minimal; the Statistics tab's Cores block and the tracked bounds on the timeline tell the story. Open the replay

Exercises that check themselves

A project with an exercise block (a task, instances with expected counts, audits and budgets) gets a Check exercise button; node tools/grade.mjs submissions/ --exercise reference.json grades a folder of submissions against the reference's instances, so a submission cannot weaken what it is graded against. The library group Exercises has two refinement starters and two loops to build from scratch, each in two parts, with a readme that guides: implicit hitting sets (exercise-ihs-seating, chapter 14) and CEGIS (exercise-cegis-voting, chapter 16); the fresh-problem exercises of chapters 5–12 come with blocking starters that make good graded exercises once you add an exercise block.
