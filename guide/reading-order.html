Reading the guide and the tutorial together

The guide teaches the design of a loop; the playground's Tutorial tab teaches the tool. This page interleaves them, so that each tool appears when a design question calls for it.

  1. Tutorial lessons 1–4: what CEGAR is, the first run, fragments and solvers, reading the trace.
  2. Guide chapters 1–4: why a loop, blocking, learning, soundness.
  3. Tutorial lesson 6 (the audit, and at its end the Refinery) and the how-to hunting an unsound refinement.
  4. Guide chapter 5, then tutorial lesson 5 (the Observatory) and the how-to finding where the time goes.
  5. Guide chapters 6–8, with tutorial lesson 9 (loop shapes).
  6. Tutorial lessons 7–8 (instances, campaigns) and the how-tos building an instance set and comparing strategies.
  7. Guide chapters 9–11, with tutorial lesson 10 (the loop language).
  8. Guide chapter 12 and tutorial lessons 11–12 (certificates and recordings, your own problem), with the how-tos checking a loop and presenting a run.
  9. Part II: chapters 13–22, the how-to turning a paper into a loop, and the bibliography.
  10. A project: a library example tagged project (its readme has a question, a baseline and things to try), headless with bench and report.
