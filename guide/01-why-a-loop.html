The problem

Delete at most k nodes of a graph so that no triangle is left. Here is the graph of the first chapters, with nodes 4 and 8 deleted: the triangle 1, 2, 3 survives.

The check

The check looks for a triangle that no deletion touches:

3 { q(V) : node(V) } 3.
:- q(V), del(V).
:- q(U), q(V), U < V, not edge(U,V).
